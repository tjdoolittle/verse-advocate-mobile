## App name (max 30 chars)

Verse & Advocate

## Short description (max 80 chars)

State your case. Instantly find the Bible verses that back up your argument.

## Full description (max 4000 chars)

Making a point and need Scripture to back it up? Verse & Advocate turns your
argument into a ranked list of supporting Bible verses — in milliseconds,
entirely on your device.

Type your case in plain English:

• "forgiveness is better than revenge" → Matthew 6:14–15
• "money is the root of all evil" → 1 Timothy 6:10
• "pride comes before a fall" → Proverbs 16:18
• "don't worry about the future" → Matthew 6:34

HOW IT WORKS

Verse & Advocate understands everyday language and maps it to biblical
vocabulary: search "revenge" and it also finds "vengeance"; search "worry" and
it finds "take no thought." Results are ranked to favor short, quotable verses
— the ones that land in a conversation — and each result shows exactly which
words matched.

FEATURES

• Plain-English topic search across the whole Bible
• Three translations: World English Bible (modern English), King James
  Version (1611), and Tyndale (1534)
• One-tap "Copy verse" — verse, reference, and translation,
  ready to paste anywhere
• Show context: read the surrounding verses so you never quote out of context
• Old Testament / New Testament filters
• Topic suggestions when you need inspiration — shuffle for new ideas
• Works completely offline — all Scripture is bundled in the app

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no network calls. Your searches never leave
your phone. The full privacy policy is one sentence long: this app collects no
data.

A NOTE ON HONEST ARGUING

Verse & Advocate finds verses about your topic — it can't tell you which side
of the argument a verse actually supports. That's what "Show context" is for.
Read before you quote; your debate opponent certainly will.
